Cold email checker

Paste the email. It checks spam phrases, lines that sound like AI, broken merge fields and spintax, length, links and reading level as you type. It also previews random variants and converts spintax between Smartlead, Instantly and PlusVibe.

      What it checks
      
      
      
        
      Syntax
      Unclosed {{ or {, a stray brace, spintax with one option, single braces in Instantly or PlusVibe copy (they print as-is), empty variables, and [First Name] placeholders left in.
      
        
      Deliverability
      Spam phrases, with the subject held to a stricter standard. Also links (more than one fails), images, attachments, Re: or Fwd: on a first email, and copy with no spintax.
      
        
      Voice
      Openers that delay the point ("I hope this finds you well", "I wanted to reach out"), "It's not X, it's Y", "Here's the thing", em dashes, corporate words, and more "I" than "you".
      
        
      Shape
      Word count across every spintax variant (50 to 100 is the target), a question to reply to, paragraph length, reading grade, and exclamation marks.
      
        
      Conversion
      Smartlead {a|b}, Instantly {{RANDOM | a | b}} and PlusVibe {{random|a|b}}, with variable names, fallbacks, custom fields and sender tags mapped. Anything that doesn't carry over is listed for you to fix, not dropped silently.
      
      
      
      
      Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
